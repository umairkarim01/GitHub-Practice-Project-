# IFRS 18 – Presentation and Disclosure in Financial Statements: One-Page Brief

Nov 22, 2026 · Umair Karim

## Overview

IFRS 18 replaces IAS 1 *Presentation of Financial Statements* for annual periods beginning on or after 1 January 2027, with earlier application permitted. The IASB issued it in April 2024 to make the income statement more comparable across companies and to give investors more transparent information on performance. Recognition and measurement are unaffected; the change is about how results are presented and explained.

## Key requirements

The most visible change is a structured income statement with defined categories and subtotals.

| Area | What IFRS 18 requires |
| --- | --- |
| Structured income statement | Income and expenses classified into five categories: **operating**, **investing**, **financing**, **income taxes** and **discontinued operations**. Operating is the default category; classification also depends on the entity's main business activities (e.g. banks, insurers, investment entities). |
| New mandatory subtotals | **Operating profit or loss** and **profit or loss before financing and income taxes** must be presented, alongside profit or loss. |
| Management-defined performance measures (MPMs) | Non-GAAP subtotals used in public communications (e.g. adjusted EBITDA) must be disclosed in a single note: why they are useful, how they are calculated, and a reconciliation to the nearest IFRS subtotal, with tax and NCI effects of each reconciling item. |
| Aggregation and disaggregation | Enhanced principles on grouping items by shared characteristics; material information must not be obscured. Labels such as "other" are discouraged unless more precise wording is unavailable. |
| Operating expenses by nature | Entities presenting expenses by function must disclose, in one note, depreciation, amortisation, employee benefits, impairment losses and inventory write-downs. |

## What stays the same, and related amendments

Much of IAS 1 carries over unchanged, including going concern, accrual basis, materiality, comparatives and the statements of financial position and changes in equity. Some IAS 1 paragraphs move to IAS 8 (renamed *Basis of Preparation of Financial Statements*) and IFRS 7.

- **IAS 7:** operating profit becomes the single starting point for the indirect method, and options for classifying interest and dividends are largely removed for most entities.
- **IAS 33:** additional per-share amounts may only use IFRS totals/subtotals or an MPM.
- **IAS 34:** MPM disclosures also apply in interim financial statements.

## Transition and adoption in Pakistan

IFRS 18 applies **retrospectively**, so comparatives must be restated. For a December year-end company, the 2026 comparative year is already being reported under the new structure in the 2027 financial statements. On transition, a reconciliation of each line item from IAS 1 presentation to IFRS 18 is required for the comparative period (and in the first interim reports under IAS 34).

In Pakistan, SECP notified adoption of IFRS 18 (together with IFRS 19) through [SRO 2444(I)/2025](https://www.secp.gov.pk/ur/document/sro-2444-of-2025-adoption-of-ifrs-18-and-ifrs-19/), published in December 2025; KPMG Pakistan's [Q4 2025 newsletter](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/pk/pdf/2026/Pakistan_MESAC_Accounting_Newsletter_Q4_2025.pdf) also reports the notification. ## Action points for preparers

- [ ] Map the chart of accounts to the five IFRS 18 categories and assess the entity's main business activities.
- [ ] Restate the 2026 income statement in IFRS 18 format and prepare the transition reconciliation.
- [ ] Identify all MPMs used in investor presentations, directors' reports and announcements; draft the MPM note.
- [ ] Update ERP/consolidation tagging, reporting templates and IAS 7 cash-flow workings.
- [ ] Review loan covenants, KPIs and incentive plans tied to "operating profit" for definitional changes.
- [ ] Brief the audit committee, board and auditors; communicate expected presentation changes to analysts.
